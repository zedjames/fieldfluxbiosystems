Program

A quantum sector is carried from operator law to gravitational ownership.

The sequence begins with the critical Schwarzschild scalar operator and its Friedrichs-selected terminal law. That choice is propagated into continuum Fock dynamics and then into action-derived local quantum matter. The geometry is next required to own the same matter law self-consistently rather than importing it from a background.

The middle papers move upstream into action and gauge structure. The self-consistent Einstein–scalar sector is bound to a Palatini master action, a square-zero BRST representation, and a regulated BV/QME program whose finite quantum complexes expose both positive substructures and explicit obstruction surfaces.

The eighth paper then separates two relations that gravity can otherwise tempt us to identify: physical ownership and gravitational visibility. Their quotient structures are globally incomparable, while intrinsic curvature data rigidly recover the source parameter on the realized nonlinear family.

When a quantum matter sector backreacts on geometry, which structures genuinely belong to the same physical owner, and which equivalences are visible only to gravity?
