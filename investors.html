The thesis

A definition creates the question. Instruments create measurable evidence.

Formal health research opens a new category of measurement questions. Our business focuses on the software, physical sensors, calibration, and instrument validation needed to bring selected applications into practice.

The broader mathematical research is stewarded through Institute Lux Consilio. FFB's commercial work includes developing measurement architectures, applications, source-witnessed instruments, and an empirical evidence program. Competitive differentiation rests on the systems we implement and validate. Read the public foundational paper →

02 · The IP

A patent-pending instrument

QPCI — Quantum Phase-Coherence Imaging — covers a measurement architecture that runs identically from an iPhone to a hospital array. Filed as a non-provisional in June 2026, on a June 2025 priority date, with 133 independent and dependent claims.

03 · The product

A live consumer app

Membrane Health is available in the App Store and uses Apple Health measurements. Instrumentation for deeper physical measurement and prospective validation is being developed through separate programs.

Risk posture

Measurement, not diagnosis — on purpose.

We form images, maps, and fields of the body's response. We do not output diagnoses or treatment decisions.

That discipline is a strategic choice as much as an ethical one: it keeps the near-term product in a defensible regulatory position while the clinical instruments mature, and it keeps every claim we make honest and auditable. Our standards →
