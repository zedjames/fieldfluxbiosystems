What differentiates the approach

A definition creates the question. Engineering makes it observable.

The published formal-health research establishes a rigorous way to state prospective health and the information needed to answer declared health questions. Fieldflux develops the physical sensing, acquisition, calibration, and software needed to investigate practical applications.

Evidence and scientific scope

Claims grow with the quality of their evidence.

Machine-checked mathematical results establish consequences of a declared formal specification. Physical measurement requires source, timing, calibration, provenance, and uncertainty controls. Clinical claims require their own empirical evidence and evaluation.

Fieldflux's current products and developing instruments are presented according to these different levels of authority.
