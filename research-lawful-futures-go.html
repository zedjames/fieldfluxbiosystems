Lawful Futures in Go

Dynamically Sufficient Observation, Historical Information, and Constitutive Health

Zed James

November 7, 2026DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24044494

Scientific context

Health, Formally Defined asks which responses an organization can continue to realize under its declared circumstances. This companion study asks a closely related question: which information must an observer keep to determine the lawful responses after the system changes?

Go makes the difference between present appearance and future permission tangible. Equal board configurations can have different lawful moves because the game's history matters. A finite-horizon request observer retains just those historical event memberships that subsequent actions may test and supports an exact abstract update. Under specified factorization assumptions, the same construction preserves complete viable-response capacities and prospective Health judgments.

The analysis establishes finite Go results, including a seven-to-twelve-class transition refinement and an exact one-bit requirement among twenty historical candidates on a six-history 3 × 3 fiber. A separate protected exchange-and-repair model demonstrates the formal result in another constituted system. These results concern declared mathematical dynamics, not empirical validation of a physiological device.
