Scientific exchange

A scientific partnership built for translation.

Founded by the same scientist for distinct purposes, Institute Lux Consilio and Fieldflux Biosystems partner across fundamental scientific inquiry and the engineering of health-measurement applications. ILC is the nonprofit steward of the broader scientific program. FFB develops the specialized commercial technology pathway.

Formal results can guide measurement design; experiments, calibration, and engineering may in turn generate questions and evidence for continued research. The scientific record retains its original authorship, affiliation, publication, and ownership information. Their research and commercialization relationship connects scientific questions with applications while preserving the distinct missions of both institutions.
