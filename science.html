Fieldflux Biosystems and Institute Lux Consilio have distinct institutional missions. ILC is an independently governed nonprofit research institute pursuing foundational questions across many fields. Fieldflux is a separately governed commercial company developing health-measurement technologies informed by related mathematical and physical research.

Formal results can guide measurement design; experiments, calibration, and engineering may in turn generate questions and evidence for continued research. The scientific record retains its original authorship, affiliation, publication, and ownership information. Any specific sponsored research, licensing, or technology-transfer relationship depends on separately documented agreements.
